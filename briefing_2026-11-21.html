How to read this

  • Picks: a screen, not a recommendation. Model rank 1 or 2, best price at least 1.10× the model's fair price, and backing that counts. At most 3, one per race. The rule has not been forward-tested.

  • ■ Model vs ■ Market: the model's win chance against the chance implied by the best price, on one scale per race.
