   edge_pct is computed exactly as the task spec defines it: (model_price / market_odds - 1), scale
   d to percent (export_briefing_html expects percent units). BUT the spec's English label ('Positi
   ve = model thinks overpriced (value)') is INVERTED relative to both the arithmetic and the codeb
   ase's own fair_vs_sp convention (backtest_base: 'Positive = model thinks horse is worse than SP'

   orse is SHORTER than the model thinks it should be = NO value. Setting value_flag on edge_pct >

   UE-value direction (edge_pct <= -5.0: market price longer than model fair price) and left edge_p
   ct itself untouched. DECISION FOR ELLIOT: either fix the spec's wording, or flip value_flag back
    — but the two cannot both stand. Note export_briefing_html also colours positive edge GREEN, wh
   ich inherits the same bug.
