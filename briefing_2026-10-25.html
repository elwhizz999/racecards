  • Picks: the model ranks it 1st or 2nd, the best price is at least 10% over the model's fair price, and someone who counts backs it. At most 3, one per race.
  • Solid chips count as backing. Outlined chips are shown for reading only. ATR's race-by-race guides tip almost every race and track the favourite, and one paper's nap on its own is weak. ★ Gold marks a trusted judge.
  • Respected: backed by people who count and rated by the model, but not a pick. The reason is shown under each one, usually that the price is too short.
  • ■ Model vs ■ Market: the model's win chance against the chance implied by the best price, on one scale per race. Model longer than market means value.
  • Trainer notes are the trainer talking about their own runner: worth reading for tone, never counted as a tip.
  • Value = best price ÷ model fair price. Green is 1.10× or better.
  • Skip: the tipsters are split and the model is unsure, or it's a big field with no agreement.
